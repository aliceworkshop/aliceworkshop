      <h4><a class="sln" href="https://cse.msu.edu/~dolsonem/" target="_blank">Emily Dolson</a></h4>
      <p>Emily Dolson is Assistant Professor at <a href="https://engineering.msu.edu/directory/faculty/dolsonem" target="_blank">Michigan State University</a>, with appointments in Computer Science &amp; Engineering and in Ecology, Evolution &amp; Behavior, where she leads the <a href="https://ecodelab.com/" target="_blank">ECODE Lab</a> (Evolutionary Control of Digital Ecologies). Her work is about how to steer the course of evolution. That question shows up in cancer, in microbiomes, and inside evolutionary algorithms, and she comes at it through artificial life, building digital systems that evolve on the same principles as biological ones along with the <a href="https://github.com/emilydolson" target="_blank">open-source tools</a> for tracking what happens inside them. She received the 2019 ISAL Award for Exceptional Service to the Artificial Life Community, and serves on the board of the <a href="https://alife.org/board-of-directors/" target="_blank">International Society for Artificial Life</a>.</p>
